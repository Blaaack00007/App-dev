My Favorite Series: Black Clover

Black Clover is my favorite anime series because of its amazing magic battles and inspiring story. It follows Asta and Yuno, two orphans who dream of becoming the Wizard King. Even though Asta has no magic, he works hard and never gives up.

Main Characters
Asta – a boy with no magic but uses anti-magic swords
Yuno – Asta’s rival with great wind magic
Noelle Silva – a royal with strong water magic

“My magic is never giving up!” – Asta
